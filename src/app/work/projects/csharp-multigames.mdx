## Overview

C#Multigames brings together several small games in one project.

## Games shown

- **Sliding picture puzzle:** arrange image tiles, with move and timer counters and shuffle and pause controls.
- **Snake:** guide the snake, collect food, and track score and level.
- **Space shooter:** control a rocket against waves of enemies while keeping track of score and remaining lives.

The screenshots show gameplay from each of the three games.
