## Overview

The Car Rental System is an all-in-one platform designed to streamline rental business operations by organizing vehicles, customers, bookings, and finances. It tracks detailed vehicle profiles—including make, model, fuel type, mileage, and availability—and customer contact details and rental histories.

## Screens

- **Reservations:** handles bookings, pricing, pickup and drop-off locations, and prevents double-booking.
- **Payments and maintenance:** records payments across multiple methods, refunds, vehicle maintenance activities, and costs.
- **Returns:** captures ending mileage and extra fees, then resets a vehicle to available or queues it for servicing.
- **Analytics and security:** reports on earnings, fleet utilization, and maintenance costs, with security features to safeguard data and support legal compliance.
