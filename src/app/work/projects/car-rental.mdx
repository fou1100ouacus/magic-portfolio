## Overview

Car Rental is a management application for rental operations. The screenshots show the sign-in screen, a dashboard with booking and vehicle statistics, and management views for vehicles and bookings.

## Screens

- **Dashboard:** booking, vehicle, user, customer, return, and transaction summaries.
- **Vehicles:** a vehicle inventory with details such as make, model, year, fuel type, and plate number.
- **Bookings:** a booking list with customer, vehicle, rental dates, and pickup and drop-off locations.

The screenshots are included as a visual walkthrough of the project interface.
