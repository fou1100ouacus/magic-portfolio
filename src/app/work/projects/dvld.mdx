## Overview

The Driving License Management System is a desktop application developed in C# to manage driving licenses efficiently. It supports the issuance and administration of licenses, helping ensure that only qualified and safe drivers are allowed on the roads.

## License services

- Issue new driving licenses.
- Renew existing licenses.
- Replace lost or damaged licenses.
- Manage international driving licenses.
